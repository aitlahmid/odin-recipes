Lasagna

It's what

Lasagna (or lasagne) is a classic Italian baked dish made of wide, flat sheets of pasta interleaved with savory fillings such as meat ragù, cheese (like ricotta, mozzarella, or Parmesan), and sauces like béchamel or tomato sauce, then baked in the oven until hot and bubbly.

Ingredients

  • Onion, garlic, carrot and celery – for the flavour base, a soffrito;
  • Beef
  • Canned tomato and tomato paste
  • Red wine – for extra flavour!
  • Seasonings – beef bouillon cubes (stock cubes), bay leaves, thyme, oregano, Worcestershire sauce

Steps

  1. Smear a bit of meat sauce on the base first – stops the lasagna sheets from sliding around;
  2. top with meat sauce, bit of white sauce
  3. ay out more lasagna sheets, then top with more meat sauce and more white sauce
  4. repeat again, lasagna sheets, meat sauce then white sauce; and
  5. cover with lasagna sheets, pour over remaining white sauce then sprinkle with cheese.
